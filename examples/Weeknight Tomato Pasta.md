# Weeknight Tomato Pasta

```recipe-ingredients
8 oz spaghetti
2 tbsp olive oil
3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
400 g canned tomatoes, crushed
1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
1/2 cup basil leaves, torn
2 tbsp butter
salt, to taste
parmesan, to taste
```

## Steps

1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water.
2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil over medium heat and cook the garlic until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and pepper flakes; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Add the pasta with a splash of pasta water and toss until the sauce clings.
5. Off the heat, stir in the butter and basil. Season with salt.
6. Serve with parmesan.

## Notes

- A 28 oz can of whole tomatoes, crushed by hand, works in place of the 400 g can — just simmer a little longer.
