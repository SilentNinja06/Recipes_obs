# Blueberry Pancakes

```recipe-ingredients
# Batter
2 cups all-purpose flour
2 tbsp sugar
1 tbsp baking powder
1/2 tsp salt
1 3/4 cups milk
2 large eggs
3 tbsp butter, melted

# Add-ins
1 cup blueberries, fresh or frozen
maple syrup, to taste
```

## Steps

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl.
2. In a second bowl, whisk the milk, eggs, and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir until just combined — a few lumps are fine.
4. Fold in the blueberries.
5. Cook on a buttered griddle over medium heat, about 2–3 minutes per side, flipping when bubbles form.
6. Serve warm with maple syrup.

## Notes

- Swap half the flour for whole wheat for a heartier pancake.
- Frozen blueberries work fine — don't thaw them first or they'll bleed.
